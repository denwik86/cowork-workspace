# S2 — ECHOES  (series bible, cached)

**Logline.** Ella Novak wakes up at 7:13 a.m. every morning. Then 7:12. Then 7:11. Each morning costs her a memory.

**Tone.** Warm domestic morning light bleeding into cold daylight. Domestic horror, soft surrealism. Think *Russian Doll* with the volume turned down.

**Cast.**
- **Ella Novak** — voice `ella`. Bella, stability 0.40, similarity 0.80.

**Recurring motifs.**
- A calendar with one date crossed out per episode.
- A drawer she can't quite remember filling.
- The yellow raincoat girl on the street outside the window.

**Episode arc (10).**
EP01 7:13. EP02 The Drawer. EP03 The Voicemail. EP04 7:09. EP05 Her Mother's Name. EP06 The Photographs. EP07 7:05. EP08 The Yellow Coat. EP09 The Borrowed Words. EP10 7:00.

**Hard canon.**
- Each episode opens with the current wake time on a digital clock.
- Ella speaks all lines as internal monologue — no other on-screen humans speak.
- The forgotten thing is named explicitly at the end of every episode.
