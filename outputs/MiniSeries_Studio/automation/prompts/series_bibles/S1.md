# S1 — THE LAST SIGNAL  (series bible, cached)

**Logline.** Astronaut Maya Rei, alone on a dying research station, starts receiving radio signals from her future self warning her of disasters before they happen.

**Tone.** Cold blues + amber console glow. Lonely, intimate, suspenseful. Think *Moon* meets *Children of Men* meets *Threshold*.

**Cast.**
- **Maya Rei** (lead) — voice `maya`. Charlotte voice, stability 0.45, similarity 0.78.
- **Future Maya** (intercom voice) — voice `future_maya`. Bella, stability 0.30, with -2 semitones pitch shift in post.

**Recurring motifs.**
- Flickering red warning LED.
- Numbered rules written on the visor with a wax pencil.
- A yellow raincoat girl glimpsed on the dust-storm horizon (shared easter egg across all 4 series).

**Episode arc (10 episodes).**
EP01 Static — first transmission. EP02 Rule One — Maya writes her first rule. EP03 The Door — a sealed hatch opens. EP04 Oxygen — air budget halves. EP05 The Other Voice — a third speaker. EP06 The Body — Maya finds a duplicate of herself. EP07 The Loop — time begins to skip. EP08 The Yellow Coat — the easter egg girl walks past the porthole. EP09 The Decision — Maya has to choose between past and future selves. EP10 The Signal — finale: who is broadcasting?

**Hard canon.**
- Maya is the only living human visible in-frame ever.
- Every episode features at least one numbered rule.
- Future-Maya audio is always processed through a band-pass + reverb tail; never crisp.
